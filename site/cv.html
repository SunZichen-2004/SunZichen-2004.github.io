MACE foundation models and ML/MM free-energy calculations

Current

Current research · Gábor Csányi Group · University of Cambridge

  • Developing MACE foundation models and ML/MM methods for free-energy perturbation calculations involving metal-binding and covalent drugs.

Subspace Fokker–Planck Transformer

October 2025 – October 2026

Research project · Yi Qin Gao Group, in collaboration with Jun Zhang · Peking University / Westlake University

  • Developed an ensemble-based framework for accelerated free-energy sampling in a selected collective-variable subspace while retaining underdamped Langevin dynamics.
  • Analyzed the self-consistent stationary distribution and convergence, and evaluated free-energy reconstruction for alanine peptides and an ML/MM Claisen rearrangement.

Barycenter-augmented MACE

September – November 2025

Research assistant · Gábor Csányi Group · University of Cambridge

  • Proposed and implemented smooth local barycenter features to enrich many-body information in equivariant neural network potentials at low computational cost.
  • Benchmarked low-correlation models on 3BPA and tested four-body counterexamples to study representation expressiveness.
  • Collaborated with Ilyes Batatia and other group members on discrimination–smoothness trade-offs.

AM1NN: neural-network-augmented semiempirical quantum chemistry

May – September 2025

Research intern · Jiali Gao Group · Shenzhen Bay Laboratory

  • Implemented a differentiable, GPU-enabled AM1 model in PyTorch, reproducing MOPAC results.
  • Designed equivariant neural corrections to electron-repulsion and resonance integrals.
  • Evaluated energies and forces on SPICE molecules and transition states, and integrated QM/MM coupling.

Mechanical-embedding ML/MM for enzyme catalysis

September 2024 – June 2025

Undergraduate research · Yiqin Gao Group, Peking University / Wen Jun Xie Group, University of Florida

  • Developed a lightweight ML/MM protocol and active-learning workflow for chorismate mutase mutations.
  • Used umbrella sampling and metadynamics to compute activation free energies, examining pocket flexibility and crystallographic waters.
  • Benchmarked mutation-dependent activation free energies against experiment, identifying stronger quantitative performance for nonpolar mutations and limitations for polar mutations.

AI-assisted design of TadA-based cytosine base editors

June – September 2024

Research intern · Weixin Tang Lab, University of Chicago / Wen Jun Xie Group, University of Florida

  • Designed scaffolds with ProteinMPNN, filtered candidates with AlphaFold2, and grafted experimentally evolved loops.
  • Constructed approximately 80 variants and tested activity in HEK cells by NGS; one variant showed clear C-editing activity.

Photocatalytic nitrogen reduction with metal-organic layers

March 2023 – June 2024

Undergraduate research · Guangxu Lan Lab · Peking University

  • Investigated nitrogenase-inspired polymetallic active centers for photocatalytic nitrogen reduction.
  • Developed a reproducible ammonia-detection protocol for catalyst screening.
